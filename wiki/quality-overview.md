# Quality overview

This page shows how carefully work has been reviewed in your organisation over a period you choose. Use it to see whether reviewers are reading properly, how busy they are, and where work is piling up.

**Who can use this:** People with the oversight lead role. Admins have this too.

**Where to find it:** In the menu on the left, under **Quality**, select **Quality overview**.

## How to change the period

1. Find the drop-down list at the top right of the page. It starts on **Last 30 days**.
2. Choose **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days** or **Last 365 days**.
3. The figures reload. While they do, the line under the title shows **Updating…**.

The line under the title always tells you the period you are looking at, for example "How carefully work has been reviewed over the last 30 days."

One panel does not follow the period: **Reviewer time used today** is always about today.

## How to read the four headline figures

The four boxes across the top give you the quick picture. Each has a small line of extra detail underneath.

| Figure | What it means | Detail underneath |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Planted mistakes caught** | How often reviewers spotted a mistake added on purpose. Higher is better. | – |
| **Typical review quality score** | The middle review quality score for the period. | How many items were released below the minimum score. |
| **Fast approvals** | The share of approvals that were made very quickly. A high figure can mean work is being approved without being read. | How many approvals took under 30% of the expected time, out of all approvals. |
| **Spot checks that disagreed** | The share of spot checks where the second reviewer reached a different outcome from the first. | How many spot checks disagreed, out of all spot checks. |

A dash (**–**) means there is nothing to measure yet for that figure.

## How to see where planted mistakes are being missed

Below the headline figures are five panels. Each one splits the **Planted mistakes caught** figure a different way:

- **Planted mistakes caught, by reviewer**
- **Planted mistakes caught, by team**
- **Planted mistakes caught, by difficulty**
- **Planted mistakes caught, by workflow**
- **Planted mistakes caught, by week**

To read a panel:

1. Find the row you are interested in, for example a reviewer's name.
2. Look at the bar and the percentage next to it. This is the share of planted mistakes that were caught.
3. Look at the two numbers on the right, for example **7/10**. The first is how many were caught. The second is how many were checked.

A low percentage based on only one or two planted mistakes tells you very little. Check the numbers on the right before you draw a conclusion.

## How to check how busy reviewers are

1. Go to the **Reviewer time used today** panel.
2. Each team has a line showing the team name, the number of reviewers, and the minutes used out of the minutes available today (for example "40 of 240 min").
3. The bar underneath shows the same thing as a percentage.
4. Under the teams, three counts show where work is stuck or has left the normal path:

| Line | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Waiting for a free reviewer** | Work that cannot be given to anyone yet because reviewers are fully booked. |
| **Sent back to where it came from** | Work that was returned without being reviewed. |
| **Escalated** | Work that has been escalated. |

You can deal with waiting work on the [On hold](on-hold.md) page.

## How to check how long reviews take

1. Go to the **Review time** panel.
2. **Typical time spent reviewing** is the middle amount of time reviewers actively spent on an item. It is shown in seconds, for example **95s**.
3. **Typical time expected** is the middle amount of time the app expected a review to take, also in seconds.

If the time spent is far below the time expected, compare it with the **Fast approvals** figure at the top.

## How to read the spread of review quality scores

1. In the **Review time** panel, find **Spread of review quality scores**.
2. Each bar is a band of scores. The number under a bar is where that band starts.
3. A taller bar means more reviews had a score in that band.
4. Hold your mouse pointer over a bar to see the band and the exact number of reviews in it.

## How to see how much work is at each stage

1. Go to the **Work by status** panel at the bottom of the page.
2. Each label shows a status and the number of items in it, for example "approved: 42".

## Common questions

**A panel says "No planted mistakes checked yet."**
No planted mistakes have been checked for that group in the period you chose. Try a longer period.

**The "Reviewer time used today" panel says "No teams set up yet."**
There are no teams yet. Teams are set up in Settings; see [People & teams](settings-people-and-teams.md).

**I see an error message and a "Retry" button instead of figures.**
The figures could not be loaded. Select **Retry**. You can also choose a different period. The page does not show figures from another period while there is an error, so you will not be misled by old numbers.

**The page says "Loading…".**
The figures are being fetched. Wait a moment.

**Where can I see more detail?**
See [Reports](reports.md) for trends, outcomes and reviewer performance, and [Review records](review-records.md) for the proof behind an individual decision.
