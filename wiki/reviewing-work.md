# Reviewing work

**To review** lists the work assigned to you. Opening an item takes you to the review screen, where you read the document, check the warnings, add comments and record your decision.

**Who can use this:** Reviewers.

**Where to find it:** Left menu, **Review** section, **To review**.

## How to see what is waiting for you

1. Select **To review** in the left menu.
2. The list shows the work assigned to you, sized to fit your review time for today.
3. Under the list, a line shows the number of items and about how many review minutes they add up to.
4. Select **Refresh** to check for new work.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Item** | The title, the kind of document, and either a **new** badge or "opened" if you have already opened it. |
| **Workflow · stage** | The workflow the work is going through and the stage you are reviewing. |
| **Risk** | The risk level given to the work: low, medium or high. |
| **Origin** | Who wrote it: ai, human or mixed. |
| **Time needed** | About how many minutes the review should take. |
| **Due** | When the review is due. A dash means no due date. |

## How to open an item

1. Select anywhere on its row.
2. The review screen opens. The document is on the left and the review panel is on the right.

The top of the screen shows the title, the risk level, who wrote it, the workflow, the stage and about how many minutes it should take. A **revision of …** badge means it is a revised version of work that was sent before. To leave without deciding, select **← Back to queue**.

## How to read the document

1. Scroll through the document on the left. Pages are shown as pages (**Page 1**, **Page 2**…), slides as slides (**Slide 1**…, with any **Speaker notes** underneath), and other documents as one page of text.
2. Read all of it. The app measures how long you actively read and how many sections you have viewed (see "Your review so far" below).
3. Select any passage to work with it. With the keyboard, press Tab to move to a passage and Enter or Space to select it.

Passages that carry a warning, a comment of yours, or that you have cited in your decision are marked in the document. Holding the pointer over a marked passage shows what the warning is about.

## How to check the warnings

The **Where to look** card lists the warnings (flags) raised on this document, with a count. Each one shows how serious it is (high, medium or low), what it is about, and a short explanation. Some carry a **required** badge.

1. Select a warning. The document scrolls to the passage and the warning is recorded as opened.
2. Read the passage.
3. Select **Acknowledge** on that warning. It then shows **Acknowledged**.

If you see "… high-risk flag(s) must be opened and acknowledged before you can approve", you must open and acknowledge every **required** warning before **Approve** will work.

If the card says "No risk flags were raised. Read the whole document.", there are no warnings to guide you, so read everything.

If an **Earlier stages** card appears, it shows what was decided at earlier stages of the workflow and the reason given.

## How to comment on a passage

1. Select the passage in the document. It appears under **Selected passage**.
2. Make sure **Comment** is selected.
3. Type in the **Your comment** box.
4. Select **Add**.

Your comment appears under **Your comments**. The person who sent the work can see it once the decision is released.

## How to suggest an edit

1. Select the passage.
2. Select **Suggest edit**. A second box, **Suggested text**, is filled with the passage's current wording.
3. Change the wording in **Suggested text**.
4. Say why in the **Why this change?** box.
5. Select **Add**.

Suggested edits appear under **Your comments** with an **edit** badge and the new wording after an arrow.

## How to remove a comment or suggested edit

1. Find it under **Your comments**.
2. Select **Remove**.

## How to use reference documents

Reference documents are source material sent with the work, such as a client file. Use them to check claims in the document. They are not part of what you are approving.

When the work has any, a badge at the top shows how many, and the panel has two tabs: **Review** and **References**.

1. Select the **References (…)** tab.
2. Open **Extracted text** under a file name to read its text in the panel.
3. Select **Download** to get the original file.
4. Select **Review** to go back to the warnings, comments and decision.

Time spent reading reference documents does not count as reading the document under review.

## How to make your decision

1. Go to the **Decision** card.
2. Choose **Approve**, **Request changes** or **Reject**.
3. Type in the box below. For **Approve** it reads **Notes (optional)**. For **Request changes** and **Reject** it reads **Reason (required)** and you must fill it in.
4. If you want to point to particular passages, select each one and choose **Cite in decision** under **Selected passage**. The card shows "Citing … passage(s)". Select **Uncite** to take one out.
5. Select **Submit decision**.

**Submit decision** stays greyed out when:

- you chose **Approve** and a **required** warning has not been acknowledged, or
- you chose **Request changes** or **Reject** and have not given a reason.

## What happens after you submit

- Normally the decision is recorded and you go back to the **To review** list.
- If the item contained a planted mistake, a result window appears first (see below).
- If you approve after too little reading, a notice appears: **This approval is below the minimum review standard.** It lists the reasons. You can go back and read more, or select **Approve anyway (recorded)**. That choice is kept on record. Your administrator may instead have set things up so that such an approval is refused; in that case you see a message with the reasons and must carry on reviewing.

The person who sent the work then sees the decision, your reason and your comments under [Sent by me](sent-by-me.md).

## Planted-mistake results

A planted mistake is a deliberate error added to some work to check it is being read carefully. When an item contained one, a window appears after you submit.

| Heading | What it means | Button |
|---|---|---|
| **You spotted a planted mistake** | You caught it. The window shows the planted wording, the **Original** wording where there is one, an explanation and its difficulty. | See below |
| **This review contained a planted mistake** | The mistake was not caught. The window shows the same details so you can see what it was. | See below |

- If the button reads **Continue reviewing**, your decision was not recorded. The app's message explains what happens next. The **Decision** card is cleared, and you carry on and decide again.
- If the button reads **Back to queue**, your decision was recorded and you return to the list.

Your results are listed in [My past reviews](my-past-reviews.md).

## Your review so far

The last card shows what has been measured for this item:

| Line | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Active reading** | Seconds you have spent actively reading, out of roughly how many are expected. |
| **Sections viewed** | How many sections of the document you have had on screen, out of the total. |
| **Flags acknowledged** | How many warnings you have acknowledged, out of the total. |

The bar fills as your reading time approaches the expected time. Only reading inside this page is measured: no webcam, nothing you type, and no activity elsewhere.

## Common questions

**"Nothing waiting. New work appears here when it is assigned to you."**
You have no reviews at the moment. Select **Refresh** later.

**"Restored your unsent draft from earlier in this session."**
Your unsent comment text, chosen decision, reason and cited passages are kept while the browser tab stays open, even if you reload or have to sign in again. Select **Discard draft** to clear them. The draft is cleared once your decision is recorded.

**"Couldn't refresh this task: …"**
The screen could not be updated just now. You can keep working; select **Try again**.

**I added a comment and got an error.**
Your text stays in the box, so nothing is lost. Try **Add** again.

**"No text could be extracted from this file. Download the original to read it."**
The reference document can't be shown in the panel. Use **Download**.

**The Download button is greyed out.**
The original file was removed under the retention policy. The extracted text is shown if it is still held.

**I can't select Approve's Submit decision button.**
Open and acknowledge every warning marked **required** first.
