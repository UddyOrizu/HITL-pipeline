# Sent by me

This page lists the work you've sent for review, with the decision and the review record once it's released.

**Who can use this:** Submitters.

**Where to find it:** Left menu, **Send work** section, **Sent by me**. You also land here straight after sending something.

## How to check on your work

1. Select **Sent by me** in the left menu.
2. Find the item in the list. There are 25 to a page; use **Previous** and **Next** under the table for more.
3. Look at the **Status** and **Decision** columns.

While anything on the page is still being reviewed, the list updates itself every 15 seconds and shows when it was last updated. You can also select **Refresh**.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Item** | The title (or a short reference code if you gave no title), the reference code and the risk level. "Revision of …" shows when it is a revised version of an earlier item. |
| **Status** | Where the work is now, with the current stage underneath when there is one. |
| **Decision** | "Waiting" until there is a decision, then the outcome. A **Review quality score** shows underneath when one is available. |
| **Submitted** | The date and time you sent it. |
| (last column) | Buttons for what you can do with the item. |

The statuses you can see, and filter by, are: received, processing, in review, spot check, approved, rejected, changes requested, deferred and cancelled.

## How to filter the list

1. Open the drop-down at the top that reads **All statuses**.
2. Choose a status. The list shows only items with that status.
3. Choose **All statuses** to see everything again.

## How to read the decision and the reviewer's comments

1. Select the item's row. A panel opens underneath it.
2. Read **Reason**, if the reviewer gave one.
3. Read **Reviewer comments**. Each one shows the passage it is about, the comment, and the stage and time it was made. A **suggested edit** badge marks a proposed rewording, shown after "Suggested:".
4. Select the row again to close the panel.

If there is no decision yet, the panel says so and shows the outcome once a reviewer has signed the work off.

## How to send a revised version

You can do this when the status is "changes requested" or "rejected".

1. Select **Revise and submit again** on the row, or in the opened panel.
2. **Send for review** opens, already marked as a revision with the title and workflow filled in.
3. Add the revised content and send it. The steps are in [Sending work for review](sending-work-for-review.md).

The new version appears in this list as its own item, marked "Revision of …".

## How to download the review record

1. Find an item that shows an **Evidence PDF** button.
2. Select **Evidence PDF**. A PDF of the review record is downloaded.

The button appears only once a review record has been released for that item.

## How to withdraw something you sent

You can withdraw work while its status is received, processing, in review or spot check.

1. Select **Withdraw** on the item's row.
2. The item is withdrawn straight away. There is no confirmation step, so check you have the right row first.

## Common questions

**"Submitted. It's been routed to a reviewer."**
Your work was sent successfully and its row is opened for you.

**"Nothing submitted yet."**
You have not sent anything. Select **Send for review** at the top of the page.

**"No submissions are …"**
Nothing has the status you filtered by. Choose **All statuses**.

**"No more submissions."**
You have gone past the last page. Select **Previous**.

**"Couldn't load your submissions."**
The list could not be fetched. Select **Refresh**.

**"No comments."**
A decision was made but the reviewer left no comments on individual passages.

**Why is there no Evidence PDF button on my item?**
The review record has not been released for it yet. When that happens depends on how your organisation has set things up.

**What is the Review quality score?**
A score for how carefully the review was carried out. See the [glossary](glossary.md).
