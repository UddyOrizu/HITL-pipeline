# Proof of Review: how-to guides

Proof of Review is where work is sent to be checked by a person before it goes out. Reviewers read the work and decide on it, and the app keeps a record showing that the review was done carefully.

These guides explain each part of the app, one page per section. If a word is unfamiliar, see the [glossary](glossary.md).

## Start here

- [Getting started](getting-started.md): signing in, your account, and how the menu is laid out.
- [Glossary](glossary.md): what the words used in the app mean.

## Settings

**Settings** is at the bottom of the left menu. It opens one area with a tab for each kind of setting. You only see the tabs you are allowed to use.

| Tab | What it is for | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| People & teams | Who can sign in, what they can do, and which team they are in | [People & teams](settings-people-and-teams.md) |
| Organisation | Rules that apply to your whole organisation: reviewing, keeping and deleting data, costs, email and sign-in | [Organisation](settings-organisation.md) |
| Review workflows | How each kind of work is reviewed: who reviews it, how quickly, and which checks apply | [Review workflows](settings-review-workflows.md) |
| Connected apps | The apps and tools that send work here, and where results are sent back | [Connected apps](settings-connected-apps.md) |
| All organisations | Every organisation on the platform (platform administrators only) | [All organisations](settings-all-organisations.md) |

## Who can do what

| Role | What they do |
|---|---|
| Admin | Looks after people, teams and settings. Can do everything except review work. |
| Reviewer | Reviews the work assigned to them. |
| Submitter | Sends work for review and follows the decision. Never reviews their own work. |
| Oversight lead | Watches review quality, decides on work that is on hold and sets up review workflows. |
| Auditor | Can read review records and the activity log. Can't change anything. |
| Integration owner | Sets up the apps and tools that send work here for review. |

One person can have more than one role. Your roles are shown under your name at the bottom of the left menu.
