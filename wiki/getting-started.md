# Getting started

This page explains how to sign in to Proof of Review, how to look after your own account, what each role can do, and how the menu is laid out.

**Who can use this:** Everyone.

**Where to find it:** The sign-in page opens whenever you are not signed in. Your account details are behind the **Your account** button at the bottom of the left menu.

## How to sign in

The sign-in page shows only the ways of signing in that your organisation has set up. You may see one or both of the following.

### With your work account

1. If you see a **Work email** box, type your work email address first. The app uses it to find your organisation.
2. Select **Sign in with your work account**.
3. Follow the steps from your organisation's own sign-in page. You are brought back to Proof of Review, which shows **Signing you in…** for a moment.

### With a password

1. Type your email address in **Work email**.
2. Type your password in **Password**.
3. Select **Sign in**.

When both ways are available, the password boxes sit under the words **or with a password**.

After signing in you land on the first page your role gives you. If you had opened a link to a particular page before signing in, you are taken to that page instead.

## How to choose an organisation

If your email address has accounts in more than one organisation, the app asks "Which one do you want?" after you sign in.

1. Select the name of the organisation you want to work in.
2. To go back without choosing, select **Back**.

To work in a different organisation later, sign out and sign in again.

## How to reset a forgotten password

The **Forgotten your password?** link appears only if your organisation has switched password resets on.

1. On the sign-in page, select **Forgotten your password?**
2. Type your address in **Work email** and select **Send reset link**.
3. A message confirms that, if an account with a password exists for that address, a link has been emailed. The link works for a limited time.
4. Open the link in the email. The page **Choose a new password** opens.
5. Type the new password in **New password (at least 12 characters)** and again in **Confirm new password**.
6. Select **Change password**.
7. A message confirms the change and tells you that every other session has been signed out. Select **Sign in**.

To leave without resetting, select **Back to sign in**.

## How to open your account

1. Look at the bottom of the left menu. It shows your name, your organisation and a badge for each of your roles.
2. Select **Your account**.

The **Your account** panel shows:

| Field | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Email** | The address you sign in with. You can't change it here. |
| **Organisation** | The organisation you are signed in to. |
| **Access** | Your roles. |
| **Sign-in** | How you can sign in: "single sign-on", "password", both, or "not set up". |
| **Name** | The name shown to other people. You can change it. |
| Email tick box | Tick **Email me about my work (new tasks, escalations, decisions), when my organisation has these emails switched on** to receive emails about your work. Emails are sent only if your organisation has switched them on. |

To close the panel, select **✕** or press Esc.

## How to change your name or email preference

1. Open **Your account**.
2. Change **Name**, or tick or untick the email box.
3. Select **Save**. The word **Saved.** appears.

## How to change or set a password

The button reads **Change password** if you already have a password, and **Set a password** if you only sign in with your work account. Setting a password lets you sign in without your work account too.

1. Open **Your account**.
2. Select **Change password** (or **Set a password**).
3. If you already have a password, type it in **Current password**.
4. Type the new password in **New password (at least 12 characters)** and again in **Confirm new password**.
5. Select **Save**.
6. A message confirms the password is saved. You stay signed in where you are; your other sessions are signed out. Select **Done**.

To leave without changing anything, select **Cancel**.

## How to sign out

1. Open **Your account**.
2. Select **Sign out** to sign out of this browser only.
3. Or select **Sign out everywhere** to sign out of every session on every device, including this one. You are asked to confirm first.

## What each role can do

Your roles are shown as badges at the bottom of the left menu and in **Your account**. You can have more than one. Your administrator gives you your roles.

| Role | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | Looks after people, teams and settings. Can do everything except review work. |
| **Reviewer** | Reviews the work assigned to them. Their reviews are checked with planted mistakes, reading activity and spot checks. |
| **Submitter** | Sends work for review and follows the decision. Never reviews their own work. |
| **Oversight lead** | Watches review quality, decides on work that is on hold and sets up review workflows. |
| **Auditor** | Can read review records and the activity log. Can't change anything. |
| **Integration owner** | Sets up the apps and tools that send work here for review. |

## How the left menu is organised

The menu shows only what your roles allow. Work sections are at the top.

| Section | Pages | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| **Review** | [To review](reviewing-work.md), [Spot checks](spot-checks.md), [My past reviews](my-past-reviews.md) | Reviewers |
| **Send work** | [Send for review](sending-work-for-review.md), [Sent by me](sent-by-me.md) | Submitters |
| **Quality** | [Quality overview](quality-overview.md), [On hold](on-hold.md) | Oversight leads |
| **Reports** | [Reports](reports.md), [Costs](costs.md) | Oversight leads and auditors (**Costs** is for oversight leads only) |
| **Records** | [Review records](review-records.md), [Activity log](activity-log.md) | Auditors and oversight leads |

At the bottom of the menu:

- **Settings** opens a page with tabs. You see only the tabs you may use, and no **Settings** link at all if there are none.
  - [People & teams](settings-people-and-teams.md): admins and oversight leads
  - [Organisation](settings-organisation.md): admins
  - [Review workflows](settings-review-workflows.md): oversight leads
  - [Connected apps](settings-connected-apps.md): integration owners
  - [All organisations](settings-all-organisations.md): only people who look after the whole service
- Your name, organisation and role badges.
- The **Your account** button.

Admins normally see everything in the menu except the **Review** section.

## Common questions

**"Your session ended. Sign in again to carry on where you left off."**
You were signed out after a while. Sign in again and you return to the page you were on. On the review screen, unsent comments and your draft decision are kept in the same browser tab.

**"Signing in isn't set up yet. Ask your administrator."**
No way of signing in has been switched on for you yet. Your administrator needs to do this.

**"This sign-in could not be completed (it may have expired or been opened in another tab). Start again."**
Select **Back to sign in** and try again in one tab.

**"This link is incomplete. Open the link from the email again, or ask for a new one."**
The reset link was not copied in full. Open it again from the email, or ask for a new link with **Forgotten your password?**

**"The passwords don't match."**
The two new-password boxes must be identical before the button will work.

**"You don't have access to anything yet. Ask your administrator to give you access."**
Your account exists but has no role yet.

**I see "Test mode: sign in with just your email".**
This appears only where the app is running in test mode. Type your email address and select **Continue**.

**What is "Reviewer access token (MCP)" in Your account?**
Reviewers see this section. It creates a one-time code that lets an AI assistant review on your behalf through a separate tool. Decisions made with it are recorded as coming from that tool. Most people never need it. Only use **Create token** if your administrator asks you to. The code is shown once, and **Sign out everywhere** cancels it.
