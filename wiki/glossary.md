# Glossary

The words used in Proof of Review, in plain terms.

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| Activity log | A list of everything that has happened in your organisation, newest first. Entries are linked together, so any change to the record would be detected. See [Activity log](activity-log.md). |
| Connected app | An app or tool that sends work here for review and receives the decision back. See [Connected apps](settings-connected-apps.md). |
| Connector | A ready-made link to another system, such as SharePoint, Microsoft Teams, Jira or Azure DevOps. A connector works on behalf of one of your connected apps. |
| Decision | What the reviewer concluded about a piece of work, for example approving or rejecting it. |
| On hold | Work that will not go out until a person decides what to do with it. See [On hold](on-hold.md). |
| Organisation | Your firm's own space in Proof of Review, with its own people, workflows, data and settings. |
| Planted mistake | A deliberate error added to some work to check it is being read carefully. The reviewer is told afterwards whether they spotted it. Planted mistakes are used for coaching, not discipline. |
| Reading activity | A record of how a document was read during a review: how long was spent and how much of it was looked at. It is only recorded for reviews done in this app. |
| Released | A decision that has been sent back to whoever asked for the review. |
| Review quality score | A score for how carefully a review was done. Each workflow has a minimum score. |
| Review record | The proof of how a piece of work was reviewed: who reviewed it, what they decided and the checks that applied. See [Review records](review-records.md). |
| Risk | How much care a piece of work needs: low, medium or high. |
| Role | The kind of access a person has, such as reviewer or admin. See the roles table on the [home page](README.md#who-can-do-what). |
| Spot check | A second, independent review of work that has already been reviewed. The second reviewer does not see the first reviewer's decision or comments. See [Spot checks](spot-checks.md). |
| Stage | One step of a workflow. Work passes through the stages in order. |
| Team | A group of reviewers. Work is assigned to a team's reviewers. |
| Warning | Something in a document that the reviewer is asked to look at closely. |
| Workflow | The rules for reviewing one kind of work: who reviews it, how quickly, and which checks apply. See [Review workflows](settings-review-workflows.md). |
