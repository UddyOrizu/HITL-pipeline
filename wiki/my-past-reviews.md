# My past reviews

This page lists the decisions you have made, how long you spent reading, and whether you spotted planted mistakes. It lets you see what is measured about you.

**Who can use this:** Reviewers.

**Where to find it:** Left menu, **Review** section, **My past reviews**.

## How to look through your past reviews

1. Select **My past reviews** in the left menu.
2. The table lists your decisions, newest first, 50 to a page.
3. If you have more than 50, use **Previous** and **Next** under the table. The line beside them shows which decisions you are looking at, for example "1–50 of 120".

This page is for reading only. You can't reopen or change a decision from here.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Decided** | The date and time you recorded the decision. |
| **Kind** | "primary" for an ordinary review from **To review**, or "spot check". |
| **Outcome** | Your decision: approve, request changes or reject. |
| **Time spent / expected** | Seconds you spent actively reading, then the seconds expected for that document. |
| **Amount read** | How much of the document you had on screen, as a percentage. |
| **Warnings checked** | Warnings you acknowledged out of the total, for example "2/3". |
| **Planted mistake** | **spotted** or **missed**, with the type of mistake, if the work contained one. A dash means it did not. |

A dash in any column means nothing was recorded for it.

## How to check your planted-mistake results

A planted mistake is a deliberate error added to some work to check it is being read carefully. They are used for coaching, not discipline.

1. Look at the box above the table, **Planted mistakes caught**. It shows how many you caught out of how many you were given, with a percentage.
2. Look down the **Planted mistake** column to see the result for each review.

The box appears only when at least one decision on the page had a planted mistake. When your reviews run over more than one page, the box is marked "(decisions on this page)" and counts only the decisions shown.

## Common questions

**"No decisions yet."**
You have not recorded any reviews so far. They appear here once you submit a decision in [To review](reviewing-work.md) or [Spot checks](spot-checks.md).

**"No more decisions."**
You have gone past the last page. Select **Previous**.

**"Your reviews couldn't be loaded."**
Something went wrong fetching the list. Select **Retry**.

**How is reading time measured?**
Only reading inside the review screen is measured. See "Your review so far" in [Reviewing work](reviewing-work.md).
