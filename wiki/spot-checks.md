# Spot checks

A spot check is a second, independent review of work that someone else has already reviewed. You won't see the first reviewer's decision or comments, so your view is your own.

**Who can use this:** Reviewers.

**Where to find it:** Left menu, **Review** section, **Spot checks**.

## How to see your spot checks

1. Select **Spot checks** in the left menu.
2. The list shows the spot checks assigned to you. It has the same columns as [To review](reviewing-work.md): **Item**, **Workflow · stage**, **Risk**, **Origin**, **Time needed** and **Due**.
3. Under the list, a line shows the number of items and about how many review minutes they add up to.
4. Select **Refresh** to check for new ones.

Which work is picked for a spot check is decided by the workflow your organisation has set up.

## How to do a spot check

1. Select a row to open it.
2. The review screen opens. A **blind spot check** badge at the top reminds you that this is a second review.
3. Review it exactly as you would any other item: read the document, open and acknowledge the warnings, add comments or suggested edits, then choose **Approve**, **Request changes** or **Reject** and select **Submit decision**. The steps are in [Reviewing work](reviewing-work.md).
4. After your decision is recorded you return to the **Spot checks** list.

## What is different from an ordinary review

- The screen shows a **blind spot check** badge.
- The first reviewer's decision and comments are not shown to you.
- **← Back to queue**, and the **Back to queue** button on a planted-mistake result, take you to **Spot checks** rather than **To review**.

Everything else works the same way, including the reading measurements under **Your review so far**.

## Common questions

**"Nothing waiting. New work appears here when it is assigned to you."**
You have no spot checks at the moment.

**Where do my spot checks show afterwards?**
In [My past reviews](my-past-reviews.md), with "spot check" in the **Kind** column.
